Arrangement

Built to loop for two hours

These play on repeat while I code. Build them so the tenth play still feels good.

3:12 + 4:40 + 3:36 =11:28per pass →~10×each in a 2-hour stream

InABABOut

In = intro · A = main loop · B = variation · Up = build · Peak = climax · Out = outro. A starting shape; change it if the music wants something else.

  • Start right awayTwo seconds of silence at the top sounds like the stream froze.
  • End cleanNo long fade. The last bar should hand off to the next track, or to itself, without a gap.
  • Skip one-time surprisesA big hit or a sound effect is fun once. By the tenth play it’s the only thing anyone hears.
  • Match loudnessAll three should sit at about the same level so none jumps out. If your DAW shows LUFS, aim near −14.

Sounds

Sound palette

Use

  • Warm keys: Rhodes, felt or upright piano
  • Jazz guitar: nylon string, or clean and muted
  • Soft boom-bap drums with swing
  • Round, warm bass
  • A little tape warmth and wobble
  • Strings or pads, saved for the peak

Maybe

  • A short vocal snippet Only if it stays under the music and never takes over.
  • A trace of vinyl texture Barely there. Heavy crackle is out.

Leave out

  • Vocal chops
  • Heavy vinyl crackle
  • Rain sounds
  • Rapping or singing
  • Anything sampled from a record, show or game

Plain language

Producer words used here

BPM
How fast the beat is. Tap your foot to Aruarian Dance; that’s around 93. Everything here sits between 80 and 96.
Bar
One count of four beats. Sections here are 4, 8, 16 or 24 bars long.
Swing
Pushing every other hi-hat a little late so the groove feels lazy instead of robotic. Turn it up until your head nods.
Seventh chord
A normal chord plus one more note. That extra note is most of what makes lofi sound jazzy and warm.
Sitting under
Drums and bass hold up the melody. If the drums are the first thing you notice, turn them down.
LUFS
A loudness reading. Matching it across all three beats keeps one from jumping out on stream.

Rules

Keep it ours

This is the one part that isn’t flexible. If a beat has someone else’s recording in it, my streams can get muted or struck.

  • Make every sound yourself, or use a royalty-free packPlay the keys, program the drums, or use sample packs whose license lets you release music made with them.
  • No samples from records, songs, anime, games or TVThat includes old Toonami clips. Nujabes built Aruarian Dance on a sample of Laurindo Almeida’s “The Lamp Is Low.” That’s how he worked, but it won’t fly on stream.
  • Write down what you usedA short list of any pack sounds and which pack they came from.
  • If you release these later, keep my channel clearDon’t opt them into YouTube Content ID through your distributor, or add my channel to the allowlist, so my own streams don’t get claimed.
